About

Precise dentistry, gentle hands.

Every smile is planned before it is made — scanned, photographed and designed with the patient.

Dr. Aida Goltalab Rad earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of Toronto in 2008, passed the Canadian National Board and both parts of the U.S. National Board examinations, and began her career in Toronto’s financial district and in underserved northern Ontario communities. She also taught pre-clinical endodontics and restorative dentistry at the University of Toronto’s Faculty of Dentistry.

Since 2020 she has practised as a cosmetic, surgical and implant dentist at Forever Smiles Clinic, a multidisciplinary aesthetic and implant centre in Tehran. Her focus is full-scope cosmetic work — composite and ceramic veneers, smile makeovers and facial-aesthetic harmony — alongside guided implant surgery, bone grafting and Piezo surgery.

Patients know her for careful diagnosis, honest treatment planning and a calm chairside manner, backed by strict sterilisation and infection-control protocols.
